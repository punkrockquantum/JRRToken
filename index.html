Why the token cost sits below the $3.50/1M market reference: the figure modelled here is the direct infrastructure cost of serving — GPUs, colo power, interconnect and storage at 35% utilisation. Market API prices are retail list prices that also carry provider margin, R&D, idle-fleet and multi-tenancy overhead. The spread between the two is the efficiency headroom a carefully designed deployment creates: right-sized silicon, KV-cache optimisation and DLR interconnect placement. That spread is shown as “customer value” — margin the customer keeps, or price advantage they can pass on. FLUX exists so the Digital Realty Solution Architecture team can configure and quantify that advantage per deployment.

Recommended additional efficiencies

    Model: KV cache FP16 = 0.330 MB/token (Track A), FP8 = 0.165 MB/token. Node KV budget = node VRAM − model weights (FP16, params × 2 bytes), optionally extended by KV-cache offload to flash (+30% Everpure / +25% NetApp effective budget). TTFT = interconnect RTT + CPU dispatch (8 ms standard, 3 ms Blackcore) + prefill (−10% with GPUDirect), where prefill tok/s = node FP8 PFLOPS × 40% MFU ÷ 2 × active params — MoE models prefill on active parameters only, VRAM holds total weights. Training throughput = prefill rate × 0.30 (≈3× FLOPs/token for fwd+bwd), scaled by interconnect, switching and routing efficiency. Prefill adds a quadratic attention term (\u22484 \u00d7 layers \u00d7 d_model \u00d7 ctx\u00b2, Llama-70B-calibrated) \u2014 the reason 100k-token prompts take ~a minute on a single GPU. KVA persistent KV cache (external flash only): cache hits stream prompt KV tensors at ~6 GB/s (deserialize-bound; \u00d72 with GPUDirect) + 90 ms management instead of recomputing prefill \u2014 calibrated to published FSI benchmarks (110k-token TTFT 54.5 s \u2192 4.0 s, 13.6\u00d7; local \u2248 NFS within ~5%). Cerebras CS-3 and SambaNova SN40L are alternative architectures sold as whole systems/racks; their $/hr, pod sizing and decode rates are market estimates. Weights precision scales weights GB from the FP16 baseline (FP32 2×, FP8/INT8 0.5×, FP6 0.375×, FP4 0.25×) and prefill compute (FP16 executes mixed-precision on FP8 tensor cores = today’s baseline; FP32 halves it, FP6 1.5× and FP4 2× on Blackwell-class / MI355X / rack-scale platforms — Hopper, CDNA 3 and dataflow systems cap at FP8, and the planner clamps honestly per platform). KV cache: FP16 0.330 / FP8 0.165 / FP4 0.0825 MB/token. DPUs (BlueField-3, Pensando Salina 400, IPU E2100) offload network + storage: −1–2 ms dispatch, +1.5–2% training fabric efficiency, $170–220/node-mo. QPU sidecars (Heron, Willow, Forte, Ankaa-3) are hybrid quantum-classical research adjacency — colo + cross connect value to DLR and a monthly cost, with no effect on token serving. SLURM power caps scale kW and sustained clocks (85% power ≈ 93% perf, 70% ≈ 85%); SLURM scheduling sets training duty cycle (gang + topology-aware 97%, default backfill 95%, naive FIFO 90%). The optimiser grid-searches these catalogs for the feasible configuration with the highest customer value under optional TTFT and budget targets. Utilisation 35%, PUE 1.3, colo $220/kW-mo, token market reference $3.50 per 1M, decimal GB.       Same maths as the planner: B300 node = 2,304 GB, Llama 3.3 70B weights = 140 GB, KV budget 2,164 GB/node, FP8 KV @ 8k = 1.32 GB/user → 1,639 users max per node; one node per site holds the 1,000-user global load three times over. TTFT = fabric RTT + 3 ms Blackcore dispatch + prefill, where prefill is compute-derived: 8 × 5 PFLOPS FP8 × 40% MFU ÷ 140 GFLOPs/token ≈ 114,000 tok/s → 70 ms for 8,000 tokens, −10% with GPUDirect → 63 ms. Burst maths: 114,000 ÷ 8,000 ≈ 14.3 sessions admitted per second per node; answer = 528 tokens at 96 tok/s = 5.5 s per user. The simulation ignores prefill/decode contention (chunked-prefill schedulers interleave them). Figures illustrative.

        Same physics as the planner, with deployment modifiers. Cooling sets PUE and sustained clocks: DLC holds 100% clocks at PUE 1.13 (+$18/kW-mo colo premium); air on ≥1 kW GPUs derates sustained clocks −8% at PUE 1.42; rack-scale NVL systems are DLC-only; CS-3 carries an internal water loop; SN40L DataScale racks are air-designed at 10 kW. A DLC design landing at a site without liquid runs air-cooled at −8%. OEM sets the hardware $/hr multiplier and lead time. Sites carry regional colo multipliers, last-mile RTT, ServiceFabric and DLC availability. Site pricing is user-editable per location: an all-in $/kW-mo colo rate, or a metered model where the facility base is 45% of the all-in rate plus energy at the site’s $/kWh × 730 h on PUE-adjusted draw (defaults are illustrative regional rates, e.g. Ashburn $0.07/kWh, London $0.18). Each site’s nodes are priced at that site’s own rate. Users split evenly across the region’s sites; training concentrates in the region’s first campus. All figures illustrative.
