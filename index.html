Why the token cost sits below the $3.50/1M market reference: the figure modelled here is the direct infrastructure cost of serving — GPUs, colo power, interconnect and storage at 35% utilisation. Market API prices are retail list prices that also carry provider margin, R&D, idle-fleet and multi-tenancy overhead. The spread between the two is the efficiency headroom a carefully designed deployment creates: right-sized silicon, KV-cache optimisation and DLR interconnect placement.     Model: KV cache FP16 = 0.330 MB/token (Track A), FP8 = 0.165 MB/token. Node KV budget = node VRAM − model weights (FP16, params × 2 bytes), optionally extended by KV-cache offload to flash (+30% Everpure / +25% NetApp effective budget). TTFT = interconnect RTT + CPU dispatch (8 ms standard, 3 ms Blackcore) + prefill (−10% with GPUDirect), where prefill tok/s = node FP8 PFLOPS × 40% MFU ÷ 2 × active params — MoE models prefill on active parameters only, VRAM holds total weights. Training throughput = prefill rate × 0.30 (≈3× FLOPs/token for fwd+bwd), scaled by interconnect, switching and routing efficiency. Prefill adds a quadratic attention term (\u22484 \u00d7 layers \u00d7 d_model \u00d7 ctx\u00b2, Llama-70B-calibrated) \u2014 the reason 100k-token prompts take ~a minute on a single GPU. KVA persistent KV cache (external flash only): cache hits stream prompt KV tensors at ~6 GB/s (deserialize-bound; \u00d72 with GPUDirect) + 90 ms management instead of recomputing prefill \u2014 calibrated to published FSI benchmarks (110k-token TTFT 54.5 s \u2192 4.0 s, 13.6\u00d7; local \u2248 NFS within ~5%). Cerebras CS-3 and SambaNova SN40L are alternative architectures sold as whole systems/racks; their $/hr, pod sizing and decode rates are market estimates.
